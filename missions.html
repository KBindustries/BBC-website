WHO WE ARE & WHAT WE BELIEVE.

ABOUT BEREAN BAPTIST CHURCH KRIBI

We exist to glorify God by fulfilling the great commission and the great commandments.

Our Vision

In terms of what we want to see the church and the nation look like in the years to come, "We desire to see a church and a nation saturated with the love of God and sharing that love in all actions and words for the glory of God and the progress of his people; in the church and the nation"

Our Mission

As a church, "we proclaim the gospel of Christ to inspire, edify and equip the saints for service in the city of Yaounde and beyond, with specific attention to the francophone regions of Cameroon".
